# Demo

A presentation page for showing the CRM to a prospect, led by our two unique strengths: the broadcast funnel and AI training.

Open **Demo** from the sidebar. Everything on it is sample data — nothing is sent and none of your records change. Click **Present** to show it full screen while screen-sharing (press Esc to leave).

## 1. The broadcast funnel (USP)

Shows how one broadcast becomes revenue: Audience → Sent → Delivered → Read → Replied → Qualified → Deal created → Won.

- Pick a sample campaign: **Festival offer**, **Abandoned cart** or **Webinar invite**.
- Click any stage to see what happens there and what the CRM does at that moment.
- Switch the **AI follow-up funnel** on and off to show the extra replies, deals and revenue the automatic follow-ups bring.
- The four "follow-up funnel" cards explain each automatic step: reminder for non-readers, follow-up for non-repliers, instant AI answers, and hot-lead handling.

## 2. AI training (USP)

Pick a customer question to compare an **untrained AI** answer with the answer **after Train AI**. Below it, the five training steps show how a business trains its AI in about 15 minutes. **Open Train AI** goes to the real page.

## 3. Automation in action

A phone plays a WhatsApp conversation while the matching flow steps light up: keyword trigger → menu buttons → collect details → tag and create a deal → AI answers → hand off to sales. Use **Pause**, **Play** and **Replay**.

## 4. Everything included

Cards for the rest of the CRM, and a closing slide with the 1, 7 and 15-day free trials.
