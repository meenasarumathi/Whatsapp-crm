# Notifications

Alerts for conversations a teammate assigns to you, so nothing assigned to you gets missed.

## What appears here

Whenever someone else assigns a conversation to you in the Inbox, a notification is created. The number next to **Notifications** in the sidebar is your unread count (shown as "9+" above nine).

## Using the page

- Click a notification to open that conversation.
- Unread items are marked **Unread**; opening one marks it read.
- **Mark all as read** clears the whole list at once.

If the list is empty you will see "No notifications yet".

## Desktop (browser) notifications

Separately from this page, you can get a pop-up on your computer for **every new customer message**, even while you are on another page:

1. Go to **Settings → Your profile → Browser notifications**.
2. Switch on **Notify me about new customer messages** and allow the browser prompt.
3. Click **Send test notification** to check it works.

> **Tip:** Desktop alerts only work while the CRM is open in a browser tab. If it says "Blocked in browser settings", click the lock icon next to the web address, allow notifications, and reload.
