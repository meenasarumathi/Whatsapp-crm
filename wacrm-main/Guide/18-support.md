# Support

Raise a complaint about the CRM, follow it until it is fixed, and get notified when the team resolves it.

## Who sees what

- Every user sees **Support** at the bottom of the left sidebar (next to Wallet). It is always on, even when other tabs are switched off for you.
- Users see only the tickets they raised.
- Super admin, Admin 1 and Admin 2 see every ticket from every account, under the title **Support tickets**.

## Raising a complaint (users)

1. Click **Support** in the sidebar, then **Raise a complaint**.
2. Fill in a **Subject**, pick a **Category** (Something is broken, WhatsApp / Instagram, Billing / Wallet, Login / Account, Feature request, Other) and a **Priority**.
3. Describe the problem: what happened, what you expected, and the steps to reproduce it.
4. Click **Submit**. You get a ticket number such as #1001.

## Following your ticket

- The status shows as **Open**, **In progress**, **Resolved** or **Closed**.
- Open a ticket to see the conversation with the support team and add more details.
- Every time the team replies or changes the status, you get an alert in **Notifications**. Click it to open the ticket.
- When a ticket is **Resolved**, a green box shows what the team did. Click **Yes, it's fixed** to close it, or **Still not fixed — reopen** to send it back to the team. Replying to a resolved ticket also reopens it.
- You can close your own ticket at any time with **Close this ticket**. A closed ticket can't take new replies; raise a new complaint if you need more help.

## Resolving complaints (Super admin, Admin 1, Admin 2)

1. Click **Support**. The counters at the top show how many tickets are Open, In progress, Resolved and Closed; click one to filter. **Active** shows Open and In progress together.
2. Click a ticket to see the user, their account, and the conversation.
3. Reply to ask questions or explain the fix. The user is notified.
4. Under **Update status**:
   - **Mark In progress** tells the user someone is working on it.
   - **Mark Resolved** needs a resolution note. The user reads this note, so write the cause and what you fixed.
   - **Close ticket** ends it without a resolution; **Reopen** brings a closed ticket back.

> **Tip:** Status changes are also written into the conversation, so anyone opening the ticket can see its full history.
