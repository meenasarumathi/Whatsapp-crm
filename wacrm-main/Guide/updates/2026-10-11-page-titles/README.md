# Correct page titles for the newer tabs

The title at the top of the screen now names the page you are on for every tab.

## What changed

- Token Wallet, Train AI, Demo, Flows and AI Agents used to show "Dashboard" in the top bar. They now show their own names.

## Who it affects

- Roles: everyone.
- Tabs: Token Wallet, Train AI, Demo, Flows, AI Agents.

## How to use it

1. Nothing to do — open any of these tabs and the top bar shows the right title.
