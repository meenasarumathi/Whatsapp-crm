# Test flows for free

A new **Test flow** button lets you try a flow in a sandbox chat. Nothing is sent to WhatsApp, so testing never adds to your WhatsApp bill.

## What changed

- A green **Test flow** button in the flow editor toolbar.
- A side panel with a pretend customer, the first message, and a chat where you tap buttons and type replies as the customer.
- It follows the same rules as live flows: trigger keywords, buttons and lists, saved answers, conditions, tags, retries for unmatched replies, and handoff.
- Notes in the chat show what happened at each step. Click one to highlight that step on the canvas.
- Tests use your latest edits, even before you save.

## Who it affects

- Roles: anyone who builds flows (Owner, Admin, Agent).
- Tabs: Flows → flow editor.

## How to use it

1. Open a flow and click **Test flow**.
2. Set up the pretend customer and their first message, then click **Start test**.
3. Tap buttons or type replies. Click **Restart** to try another path.
