# New look: Zyloop WACRM

The CRM now carries its own brand, Zyloop WACRM, with a look that no longer resembles other copies of the open-source template.

## What changed

- Name **Zyloop WACRM** in the sidebar, browser tab, sign-up text and messages (invites, settings).
- New logo mark (a looped "Z" on a teal-to-indigo tile), also used as the browser icon.
- New default accent **Zyloop**: electric teal, with midnight-ink dark surfaces instead of flat grey.
- New typeface (Plus Jakarta Sans) and slightly rounder corners.
- Redesigned sign-in, sign-up and password pages: a brand panel with what the CRM does next to the form.
- Active menu item shows a gradient marker.
- Everyone starts on the Zyloop accent once; other accents stay available in Settings → Appearance.

## Who it affects

- Roles: everyone
- Tabs: all (sidebar, sign-in pages, Settings → Appearance)

## How to use it

1. Nothing to do — sign in and the new look is there.
2. Prefer another colour? Settings → Appearance → **Accent color**.
