# Download and upload conversations as Excel

The inbox contact panel has a new **Data sheet** tab to download a chat as Excel or CSV, and to upload a conversation file that someone shared.

## What changed

- The contact panel now has two tabs: **Details** and **Data sheet**.
- **Download Excel (.xlsx)** or **Download CSV** of the whole conversation: date, time, sender, type, message, status, template and media link.
- **Upload Excel / CSV** opens a conversation file in a preview, from where you can **Save to contact notes** or **Teach AI** (adds the question-and-answer pairs to Train AI).

## Who it affects

- Roles: Owner, Admin, Agent (Viewers can download).
- Tabs: Inbox → contact panel.

## How to use it

1. Open a conversation in the **Inbox** (for example, a chat from Gru).
2. In the right-hand panel, click **Data sheet**.
3. Click **Download Excel** to share the chat, or **Upload Excel / CSV** to open a shared one.
