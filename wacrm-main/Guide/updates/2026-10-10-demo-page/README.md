# Demo page for showing prospects

A new **Demo** tab presents the CRM to a prospect, led by our two unique strengths: the broadcast funnel and AI training.

## What changed

- New sidebar tab **Demo** with a full-screen **Present** mode.
- An interactive **broadcast funnel**: from audience to won deals, with an on/off switch that shows the extra revenue from the AI follow-up funnel.
- An **AI training** comparison: untrained answers next to trained answers, plus the five training steps.
- **Automation in action**: a phone plays a WhatsApp chat while the flow steps light up.
- An "everything included" overview and a free-trial closing slide.

## Who it affects

- Roles: everyone (it uses sample data only).
- Tabs: Demo.

## How to use it

1. Click **Demo** in the sidebar.
2. Click **Present** before sharing your screen.
3. Walk through sections 1 to 4. Click funnel stages and switch the follow-up funnel on and off as you talk.
