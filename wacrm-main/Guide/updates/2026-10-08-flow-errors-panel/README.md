# Flows: scrollable, collapsible error panel

The error list under the flow editor no longer squeezes the editor — it scrolls, and you can hide it.

## What changed

- The error/warning panel at the bottom of the flow editor now has a fixed height (about five lines) with its own scrollbar. The editor area above keeps the rest of the screen.
- A **Hide / Show** button on the "N errors, N warnings" line collapses the panel to a single line for even more room.
- Nothing else changed: clicking an error still jumps to that node, and the counts still update as you edit.

## Who it affects

- Roles: anyone who builds flows (Owner, Admin, Agent).
- Tabs: Flows → flow editor.

## How to use it

1. Open a flow in **Flows**.
2. Scroll inside the error panel to see every issue.
3. Click **Hide** to shrink the panel while you work; click **Show** to bring it back.
