# Instagram moved to Settings

Instagram is now set up and managed in Settings → Instagram, next to WhatsApp, instead of having its own sidebar tab.

## What changed

- New Settings section **Instagram** (Workspace group): Connect & test, Automations and Inbox.
- The **Instagram** sidebar tab is gone; old `/instagram` links open Settings → Instagram.
- Instagram is no longer a separate on/off tab under Subscription users.

## Who it affects

- Roles: everyone with Settings access
- Tabs: Settings

## How to use it

1. Settings → **Instagram**.
2. **Connect & test** to connect or simulate, **Automations** to switch replies on, **Inbox** to read and reply.
