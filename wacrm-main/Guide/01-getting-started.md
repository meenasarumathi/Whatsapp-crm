# Getting started

Your first login: how to sign in, what your role allows, how the screen is laid out, and a checklist for day one.

## What this CRM does

This is a CRM built around **WhatsApp**. Customers message your business number on WhatsApp, and every conversation lands in one shared **Inbox** that your whole team can work from. Around the inbox you have:

- **Contacts** — everyone who has messaged you or that you have added.
- **Pipelines** — sales deals moving through stages (New Lead → Won).
- **Broadcasts** — bulk messages to many contacts using approved templates.
- **Automations** and **Flows** — rules and chat-bots that reply and act for you.
- **AI Agents** and **Train AI** — an optional AI assistant that drafts or sends replies, and a friendly page to teach it about your business.
- **Demo** — a presentation page to show prospects what the CRM does.
- **Dashboard** and **Notifications** — what is happening and what needs you.

## Signing in

1. Open the CRM link and you land on **Sign in** ("Welcome back").
2. Enter your **Email** and **Password**, then click **Sign in**.
3. Forgot it? Click **Forgot password?**, enter your email, click **Send reset link**, then follow the link in your inbox.

### Creating an account

- On the sign-in page click **Create account**.
- Fill in **Full name**, **Email**, **Password** (at least 6 characters) and **Confirm password**.
- Check your inbox and click the confirmation link before signing in.

### Joining a team from an invite link

If a teammate sent you an invite link:

1. Open the link. You will see "You're invited to your team name" and the role you will get.
2. If you have no account yet, click **Create account & join**. If you do, click **I already have an account** and sign in.
3. Click **Accept invitation**. You now work inside the team's account.

> **Tip:** Invite links expire (1, 7 or 30 days) and work only once. If yours says "Invite expired" or "Invite already used", ask the admin for a new one.

## Roles — what you can and cannot do

Your role is shown as a coloured chip at the bottom of the sidebar next to the account name.

| Role | What it can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Everything, including deleting the account and transferring ownership. One per account. |
| **Admin** | Everything except owner-only actions: invite/remove members, connect WhatsApp, manage templates, tags, custom fields, currency, API keys and AI setup. |
| **Agent** | Day-to-day work: reply to chats, add contacts, move deals, run broadcasts, edit automations. Cannot change workspace settings. |
| **Viewer** | Read-only. Can browse everything but cannot reply or change anything. |

If a button looks greyed out or a message says "Read-only", it is because your role does not allow that action.

### Platform roles and subscriptions

On top of the account roles above, the person who runs the CRM as a product decides who can use it and for how long (Settings → **Subscription users**):

| Platform role | What it can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Super admin** | The CRM owner. Manages everyone, creates users, makes people Admin 1 or Admin 2. Never restricted. |
| **Admin 1** | Manages subscription users: free trials, paid status, suspensions and which tabs they see. |
| **Admin 2** | Sees subscription users and can start or extend their free trials. |
| **Subscription user** | A customer using the CRM on a free trial (1, 7 or 15 days) or a paid plan. |

A subscription user only sees the sidebar tabs they have been given. When a free trial ends, the CRM shows "Your free trial has ended" until an admin marks them paid or gives a new trial. Settings always stays open so they can manage their profile and password.

## The screen layout

- **Left sidebar** — the main menu: Dashboard, Inbox, **Train AI**, Notifications, Contacts, Pipelines, Broadcasts, Automations, Flows (Beta), AI Agents, **Demo**, **Guide**, and **Token Wallet** and Settings at the bottom. You only see the tabs your plan includes.
  - On a free trial, a strip at the top of every page shows how many days are left.
  - When the token balance runs out, a red strip says automated replies are paused (manual replies keep working).
  - A pulsing dot next to **Inbox** means you have unread conversations.
  - A number next to **Notifications** is your unread notification count.
- **Bottom of the sidebar** — your name and email. Click it for **Profile**, **Settings** and **Sign out**.
- **Top bar** — the current page title, the light/dark **mode toggle**, and your avatar menu (same Profile / Settings / Sign out options).
- **On a phone** — the sidebar is hidden. Tap the **☰ menu** button at the top-left to open it.

## Day-one checklist

Work through these in order. Steps marked (admin) need an Owner or Admin.

1. **Set up your profile** — Settings → Your profile: add your display name and photo so teammates recognise you.
2. **Connect WhatsApp** (admin) — Settings → WhatsApp. Nothing is sent or received until this is done. See the Settings chapter.
3. **Sync or create message templates** (admin) — Settings → Templates. Needed to start chats and send broadcasts.
4. **Create tags and custom fields** (admin) — Settings → Fields & tags (e.g. tags "VIP", "Lead"; field "City").
5. **Invite your team** (admin) — Settings → Team members → Invite member.
6. **Import or add contacts** — Contacts → Import (CSV) or Add Contact.
7. **Create your sales pipeline** — Pipelines → Create Pipeline.
8. **Add quick replies** — Settings → Quick replies, for answers you type often.
9. **Turn on a welcome automation** — Automations → pick the "Welcome Message" template.
10. **Turn on browser notifications** — Settings → Your profile → Browser notifications.

> **Tip:** If you see a red banner saying "This user is not linked to an account" or "Could not load your permissions", nothing you change will be saved. Click **Retry**, and if it stays, ask your admin to re-send your invitation.
