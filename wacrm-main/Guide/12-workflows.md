# End-to-end workflows

How the tabs work together: complete, step-by-step journeys from first setup to closed deal.

## How everything connects

1. A customer messages your **WhatsApp** number.
2. A **contact** is created automatically and the chat appears in the **Inbox**.
3. **Flows**, **Automations** or the **AI Agent** may reply, tag or assign it first.
4. A teammate is **assigned** and gets a **Notification**.
5. The teammate replies and, if it is a sale, creates a **deal** in **Pipelines**.
6. Later you reach many contacts at once with a **Broadcast**.
7. The **Dashboard** shows the results.

---

## Workflow 1 — Setting up the CRM (admin, once)

1. **Settings → WhatsApp** — connect your number and confirm "Registered".
2. **Settings → Templates** — click **Sync from Meta** or create templates.
3. **Settings → Fields & tags** — create tags (Lead, Customer, VIP) and fields (City, Source).
4. **Settings → Deals & currency** — choose your currency.
5. **Pipelines** — create your sales pipeline and adjust its stages.
6. **Settings → Quick replies** — add common answers.
7. **Settings → Team members** — invite agents.
8. **Automations / Flows** — switch on a welcome message or menu.
9. **AI Agents** (optional) — set up, test in the Playground, then enable.

## Workflow 2 — Handling a new customer enquiry (agent, daily)

1. See the pulsing dot on **Inbox** or a desktop alert.
2. Open the chat under **Unread**.
3. **Assign** it to yourself so teammates know you are on it.
4. Reply — use a **quick reply** or **Draft with AI**.
5. In the contact panel add **tags** (e.g. "Lead") and a **note**.
6. Set status to **Pending** while waiting for the customer.
7. When it is resolved, set status to **Closed**.

## Workflow 3 — From lead to won deal (sales)

1. A lead asks about prices in the Inbox.
2. **Pipelines → Add Deal**: title, contact, value, expected close date, stage **New Lead**, link to the conversation.
3. Qualify the lead → drag the deal to **Qualified**.
4. Send the quote → drag to **Proposal Sent**.
5. Discuss terms → drag to **Negotiation**.
6. Customer agrees → open the deal → **Mark as Won**.
7. Check **Won This Month** in the pipeline and **Pipeline Value** on the Dashboard.

## Workflow 4 — Running a promotional campaign (marketing)

1. Make sure an approved **template** exists (Settings → Templates).
2. Tag the target customers in **Contacts** (or have a CSV ready).
3. **Broadcasts → New Broadcast**.
4. Template → Audience (**Filter by Tags**, e.g. "Customer", exclude "Unsubscribed") → Personalize ({{1}} = Contact **Name**) → name it → **Send Broadcast**.
5. Open the broadcast to watch **Delivered / Read / Replied**.
6. **Retry failed** for any failures, and handle replies in the Inbox.

## Workflow 5 — Re-engaging a customer after 24 hours

1. The chat timer shows **Expired**.
2. Click **Templates** in the composer (or **Send template** on the contact).
3. Choose a template, fill in the placeholders, and **Send template**.
4. Once the customer replies, the 24-hour window reopens and you can chat normally.

## Workflow 6 — Automating first replies and routing

1. **Flows → New flow → Welcome menu** template.
2. Edit the welcome text and buttons (e.g. "Sales", "Support", "Order status").
3. Make each button lead to a **Handoff to agent** node, or to more answers.
4. **Save**, fix any validation issues, then **Activate**.
5. Add an **Automation**: trigger **Keyword Match** "urgent" → **Add Tag** "Priority" → **Assign Conversation** to a senior agent.
6. Test both from your own phone, then check **Runs** and **View Logs**.

## Workflow 7 — Bringing a new teammate on board

1. Admin: **Settings → Team members → Invite member** → choose the role → **Generate link** → **Send via WhatsApp**.
2. Teammate: opens the link → **Create account & join** → **Accept invitation**.
3. Teammate: sets profile photo and turns on browser notifications.
4. Teammate: reads this **Guide**, starting with Getting started.
