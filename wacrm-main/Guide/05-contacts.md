# Contacts

Your address book: every customer and lead, with their tags, custom fields, notes, deals and history.

Contacts are created automatically when a new number messages you, or you can add and import them yourself.

## The contacts list

- **Search by name, phone, or email...** — instant search.
- **Filter by tags** — show only contacts with the chosen tags (**Clear all** to reset).
- Columns: **Name**, **Phone**, **Email**, **Company**, **Tags**, **Created**.
- Use the arrows at the bottom to page through ("Showing 1-25 of 300").

### Buttons at the top

- **Add Contact** — create one contact.
- **Import** — bring in many contacts from a CSV file.
- **Custom fields** — add or rename extra fields (same as Settings → Fields & tags).

### Bulk actions

Tick the checkboxes on the left (or the header box to select the whole page). A bar shows "N selected" with **Delete selected** and **Clear**.

## Adding a contact

1. Click **Add Contact**.
2. Fill in **Name**, **Phone Number**, **Email**, **Company** and **Tags**.
3. Click **Save Contact**.

> **Tip:** The phone number **must start with + and the country code**, e.g. +14155550123 or +919876543210. Without it, messages go to the wrong country. If the number already exists the CRM warns you and offers **View** to open the existing contact.

You can create a new tag on the spot by typing its name in **Tags** and choosing **Create tag "…"**.

## Importing from CSV

1. Click **Import**, then **Click to choose a CSV file**.
2. The file needs a **phone** column. Optional columns: **name**, **email**, **company**, **tags** (several tags separated by commas, with the cell in quotes).
3. Check the **Preview** and the "N rows ready" count.
4. Click **Import N contacts**.
5. Read the result: imported, duplicates skipped, invalid phones, failed rows.

Tags in the file that do not exist yet are skipped — create them in Settings first.

## Contact details

Click a contact's name to open its detail panel. At the top are **Edit**, **Message** and **Delete**. The tabs are:

| Tab | What you do there |
| --- | --- |
| **Details** | View and edit name, phone, email, company. **Save Changes** to keep edits. **Send template** starts a WhatsApp chat. |
| **Activity** | The history of everything that happened with this contact. |
| **Notes** | Internal notes for your team. Write one and click **Add Note**. |
| **Custom Fields** | Fill in your extra fields (e.g. City, Lead source) and click **Save Custom Fields**. **Manage fields** adds new ones. |
| **Deals** | Deals linked to this contact. |
| **Tags** | Click a tag to add or remove it. |

## Deleting

**Delete** on a row, in the detail panel, or **Delete selected** for many. Deleting cannot be undone.
