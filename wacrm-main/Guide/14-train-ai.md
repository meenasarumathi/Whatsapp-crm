# Train AI

Teach your AI assistant to answer like your best team member — in plain language, no technical skills needed.

Open **Train AI** from the sidebar (just below **Inbox**). Everything you add here is used straight away by inbox **Draft with AI**, the **auto-reply** bot and the **Playground**.

## AI readiness

The ring at the top scores your training out of 100, and the checklist beside it shows what to do next:

- Connect an AI provider (AI Agents → Setup)
- Fill in the business profile
- Add 15 example answers
- Add 3 rules
- Upload at least one document
- Switch the AI on for customers

Click any checklist line to jump to that step.

## 1. Business profile

Answer simple questions like you would brief a new team member: business name, what you sell, who your customers are, tone of voice, languages, opening hours, location, pricing, policies, what every chat should achieve, and when a human should take over. Choose the **reply length** and whether to **use emojis**, then click **Save profile**.

## 2. Q&A answers

Write a question customers really ask and the exact answer you would give, with an optional category. When a similar question comes in, the AI answers the same way.

- **Switch** — turn an answer on or off without deleting it.
- **Edit** / **Delete** — the pencil and bin icons.
- **Used N times** — how often the AI has used that answer.
- **Import Excel / CSV** — a sheet with **Question**, **Answer** and optional **Category** columns. **Download template** (or **Export to Excel**) gives you the right format.

## 3. Rules

Short "always / never" instructions, for example "Ask for the order number before discussing an existing order". Click a suggestion to start, or write your own. Rules can be switched off and edited like answers.

## 4. Learn from chats

Pick a conversation your team handled well. Each customer question and the reply that followed becomes a suggested answer. Edit the wording, untick any you don't want, and click **Save to training**.

> **Tip:** You can also teach the AI from a spreadsheet: Inbox → contact panel → **Data sheet** → **Upload** → **Teach AI**.

## 5. Documents

Upload price lists, FAQs, brochures or policies. The AI looks up the right part of a document for each question. (Uploading documents needs an Owner or Admin.)

## 6. Test it

Chat with the AI exactly as a customer would. It uses everything you have trained, and nothing is sent on WhatsApp.

## 7. What AI can do

A card for every AI feature in the CRM, with **On** / **Off** status: 24/7 auto-reply, reply drafts, smart handoff, multiple languages, answers from documents, your training, lead-qualifying flows and the broadcast funnel. Click a card to open where it is set up.

## Who can train

Owners, Admins and Agents can add and edit training. Viewers can look but not change anything.

> **Tip:** The AI-training tables are created by `db/schema.sql` (`npm run db:init`).
